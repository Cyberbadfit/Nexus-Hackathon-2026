help GUIDELINES

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Important details regarding eligibility, team size, evaluation, and hardware provisioning.

What is the maximum team size for SRM Hackathon 2026?

expand_more
Each squad has a strict maximum capacity of 4 members (1 Team Leader + up to 3 Teammates). Once 4 members are registered, the team's verification code is automatically locked by the system and cannot accept additional members.

How does the Unique Student Verification Code work?

expand_more
When the Team Leader creates the team, a unique verification code (e.g. SRM-2026-ABC123) is generated automatically. The leader shares this code with their teammates. Teammates enter this code in the "Join with Code" section to be inducted directly into the squad database.

Are hardware kits provided on-site for Track 4 (IoT & Healthcare)?

expand_more
Yes! SRM Tech Park Labs will provide ESP32 modules, biometric sensors, LoRa gateways, and soldering benches. Teams are also welcome to bring their own proprietary development boards.

What are the judging criteria?

expand_more
Prototypes are evaluated on: Technical Complexity (30%), Real-World Viability & Innovation (25%), Design & User Experience (20%), Completeness of Demo (15%), and Pitch Presentation (10%).
